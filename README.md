# NGCC Public-Key (Post-Quantum) Round 1 Tracker

Unofficial, bilingual (中文 / English) tracker for the 84 Round 1 post-quantum candidates of the NGCC call
for public-key cryptographic algorithms (34 signatures, 41 KEMs, 9 key exchange protocols): public forum
findings, submitter responses and revisions.

Site: https://ngcc26track.github.io/ngcc-pkc-tracker/ · Hash track: https://ngcc26track.github.io/ngcc-hash-tracker/

- Sources: the PKC public comment forum (list.niccs.org.cn) and the official candidate list (niccs.org.cn).
  The forum posts and official materials are authoritative.
- Finding levels, one-line summaries and the maintainers' verification reflect only the views of the site
  maintainers and may contain errors. Corrections are welcome in the relevant forum thread.

本站为非官方整理，汇总 NGCC 公钥密码算法第一轮 84 个后量子候选在 PKC 公开论坛上的评论、提交方回应与版本更新。
发现等级、一句话总结与维护方核验仅代表网站维护方观点；内容以论坛原帖与官方材料为准。
